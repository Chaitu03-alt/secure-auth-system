Account Security

  • Bcrypt Password Storage
    Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost=12) and never stored in plaintext. Each account has a unique cryptographic salt.
  • Two-Factor Authentication Setup
    After registration, scan a QR code with your authenticator app. 2FA is mandatory for all accounts.
  • CSRF Token Protection
    This form is protected with a per-session CSRF token to prevent cross-site request forgery.
