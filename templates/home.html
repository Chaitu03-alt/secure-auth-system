Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA)

RFC 6238 compliant TOTP generator compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, and 1Password.

Smart Throttling & Limiting

Sliding-window IP rate limiting prevents credential stuffing and automated brute-force attacks gracefully.

Timing-Safe Cryptography

High-entropy Bcrypt password hashing with constant-time dummy comparisons to eliminate username enumeration.

CSRF Protected Forms

Cryptographic tokens bound to every user session ensure state-changing requests cannot be forged.

Audit Event Trail

Comprehensive login history tracking client IPs and status timestamps for complete operational visibility.

Clean, human-centered responsive interface designed for readability across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
