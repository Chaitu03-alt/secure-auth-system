Verification Details

  • Replay Protection
    Each 6-digit code is single-use. Codes are invalidated immediately after consumption.
  • Clock Drift Tolerance
    A tolerance of ±1 time window (30 seconds) is allowed to account for minor clock skew between devices.
  • Lockout on Repeated Failures
    Repeated failed verification attempts trigger a server-side lockout to prevent automated attacks.

Algorithm: HMAC-SHA1 / RFC 6238
